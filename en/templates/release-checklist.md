# Release Checklist

Work / episode / version:
Target territory and platform:
Review date:

## Content and technical
- [ ] The author watched the complete candidate master
- [ ] Story, character, shot, and audio continuity pass
- [ ] Aspect ratio, duration, resolution, frame rate, codec, and filename meet current requirements
- [ ] Captions are accurate and safe; sync and dialogue intelligibility pass
- [ ] Cover, title, description, tags, rating, and warnings are accurate

## Rights and transparency
- [ ] Script, characters, images, video, voice, music, fonts, and marks are cleared
- [ ] Real-person likeness/voice and minor consent records are complete
- [ ] No unlicensed long quotation, lyric, character, image, or recognizable style copy remains
- [ ] Release-date AI visible/metadata labels were checked and required provenance was preserved
- [ ] Promotion does not fabricate views, rankings, income, reviews, awards, or partnerships

## Files and authority
- [ ] Sources, master, captions, cover, licenses, and policy snapshot are backed up
- [ ] Prompts, comments, secrets, personal data, and private metadata are removed
- [ ] The exact upload/release/payment/contract action is explicitly authorized

Final decision: release / hold / return for revision
