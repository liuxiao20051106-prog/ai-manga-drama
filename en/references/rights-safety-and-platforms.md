# Rights, Safety, and Platform Rules

Load for real-person likeness or voice, protected works, minors, third-party music/fonts, publication, or commercialization. This is a risk framework, not legal advice; laws, contracts, and platform terms vary.

## Before production

- Record the source and license for script, characters, world, images, video, voice, music, fonts, marks, and references.
- Use cloned speech only for the speaker’s own voice or with written authorization covering the intended use.
- For identifiable minors, verify guardian, privacy, platform, and local-law requirements and minimize data.
- “Downloadable,” “generatable,” or “free tier” does not establish commercial rights.
- Convert living-creator or specific-work imitation into high-level visual traits.

Use [the rights and consent log](../templates/rights-consent-log.md).

## Likeness, voice, and deception

Do not make real people appear to say or do things they did not, especially in news, politics, health, finance, disaster, or crime contexts. ElevenLabs states that it blocks celebrity and other high-risk voice cloning and verifies professional voice clones; current service terms and rights still need review: <https://elevenlabs.io/safety>

For actors, record source recordings, territories, platforms, languages, adaptations, synthesis, retraining, term, and withdrawal.

## AI labeling

Official entry points checked **2026-08-21**; reopen them on the release date:

- **China:** official measures and standards address visible and metadata labeling across text, image, audio, video, and virtual scenes: <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654685896173.htm>
- **YouTube:** creators disclose realistic, meaningfully altered or synthetic content involving people, places, events, or scenes through the upload flow: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491>
- **TikTok:** realistic AIGC requires labeling and may receive creator or automatic labels, including from content credentials: <https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content>

Animation and fantasy may be treated differently by a platform, while local law may be stricter. Preserve provenance metadata and transparent context when uncertain.

## Copyright and human authorship

Record concrete human work in scripting, storyboarding, selection, arrangement, revision, direction, and editing. The U.S. Copyright Office’s 2025 report states that AI assistance does not bar protection, but sufficient human-authored expression is required and prompts alone generally do not establish it: <https://www.copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html>

Recheck content policy, rating, labels, copyright complaints, commercial disclosures, and monetization immediately before release. A passing quality review is not authorization to publish or sign.
