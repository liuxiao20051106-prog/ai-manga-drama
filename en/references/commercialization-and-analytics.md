# Commercialization and Analytics

Load for audience positioning, serialization, revenue, platform choice, promotion, contracts, or IP development. This is a decision framework, not a view or income guarantee.

## Product and capacity

- State who watches, why they continue, what differs, and how early episodes honestly deliver the packaging promise.
- Set cadence from actual capacity, including failure, rework, review, rest, and backlog.
- Maintain episode payoff, arc turns, and long-term questions instead of mechanically copying one template.
- High-volume generation is not inherently commercial quality; human selection and creative control must remain visible.

## Metrics and experiments

Definitions differ by platform. Inspect delivery reliability, opening retention, average watch, completion, next-episode behavior, saves, comment themes, negative feedback, conversion, and unfollows.

Test one major variable at a time and record hypothesis, baseline, window, success/stop condition, and side effects. Do not buy fake engagement, impersonate viewers, or mass-comment.

YouTube’s monetization policy identifies repetitive or mass-produced low-originality work as inauthentic while allowing AI-assisted work that shows original narrative and creative value. Recheck the current page before release: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392>

## Revenue and contracts

Models may include ad/platform shares, paid episodes, membership/early access, sponsorship, commissions, licensing, and adaptations. Inspect exclusivity, territory, language, term, renewal, takedown, ownership, derivatives, sublicensing, revenue definitions, costs, accounting, tax, audit, update duties, AI disclosure, breach, and reversion.

Marketing pages do not replace contracts. High-impact rights transfers may warrant qualified local review.

Do not fabricate views, rankings, revenue, reviews, awards, or partnerships. YouTube Shorts currently supports square or vertical videos up to three minutes, with additional classification, copyright, and monetization rules: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877>
